**K. Mohammadnezhad**, et al. "Investigating heavy metals soil contamination state on the rate of stomach cancer using remote sensing spectral features." *Environmental Monitoring and Assessment*, 2023. DOI: [10.1007/s10661-023-11234-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10661-023-11234-5)

**Methodological Highlight:** Integrated multi-temporal Hyperion and Landsat imagery with meteorological data and GIS. The spatial-pattern analysis utilized remote sensing spectral features to establish statistical correlations between heavy-metal soil contamination and gastric cancer incidence in Golestan Province.
